Introduction

Hypersomnia sleep disorders are a group of chronic neurological disorders, characterized by excessive daytime sleepiness. People with hypersomnia sleep disorders experience many symptoms that affect their ability to function during the day.

Hypersomnia sleep disorders include:

  • Narcolepsy type 1 (with cataplexy)

  • Narcolepsy type 2 (without cataplexy)

  • Idiopathic Hypersomnia

  • Kleine-Levin syndrome (KLS)

The diagnosis for a sleep disorder is often a long and difficult process, including an overnight sleep study, or polysomnography, and a multiple sleep latency test, MSLT, the following day.
